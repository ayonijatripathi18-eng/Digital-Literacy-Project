# Social Media Do's and Don'ts Checklist

## Do's
- Think before posting or sharing any content online.
- Use respectful and polite language while communicating.
- Protect your privacy by adjusting account security settings.
- Verify information before sharing it with others.
- Maintain a professional tone on public platforms.

## Don'ts
- Do not share personal or sensitive information publicly.
- Avoid posting offensive, harmful, or misleading content.
- Do not engage in online arguments or cyberbullying.
- Avoid oversharing details about your personal life.
- Do not post content that could harm your academic or professional image.
