# Stay Safe Online – Cybercrime Prevention Checklist

- Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank.
- Do not scan QR codes sent by unknown people to “receive” money.
- Always verify calls or messages claiming urgency or threats.
- Use strong, unique passwords for banking and email accounts.
- Enable app locks and two-factor authentication on all financial apps.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links received via SMS, email, or social media.
- Regularly check bank statements and transaction alerts.
- Log out from financial apps on shared or public devices.

If you become a victim, report immediately:
- National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
